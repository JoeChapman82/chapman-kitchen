# Unusual Ingredients

**Theme:** Ingredients used across the Chapman Kitchen recipe collection that are not commonly found in a standard UK supermarket. Most are available from Asian supermarkets, Indian grocery shops, Middle Eastern shops or online. Many are increasingly stocked by larger Tesco, Sainsbury's, Waitrose and M&S stores.

| Middle Eastern ingredients | Middle Eastern shops, Waitrose, M&S, online |
